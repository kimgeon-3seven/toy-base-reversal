# Third-party assets

All third-party assets used by Toy Base Reversal are distributed under
Creative Commons Zero (CC0). Attribution is not required, but the creators and
source pages are documented here for traceability.

## Kenney

Creator: Kenney Vleugels / Kenney.nl  
License: [Creative Commons Zero 1.0](https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/)

- [Tower Defense (Top-Down)](https://kenney.nl/assets/tower-defense-top-down)
  - Selected tower, obstacle, unit, commander, and core sprites.
- [Particle Pack](https://kenney.nl/assets/particle-pack)
  - Selected muzzle, projectile, impact, and ability textures.
- [Smoke Particles](https://kenney.nl/assets/smoke-particles)
  - Selected smoke and explosion textures.
- [Interface Sounds](https://kenney.nl/assets/interface-sounds)
  - Selected click, confirmation, and error sounds.
- [Impact Sounds](https://kenney.nl/assets/impact-sounds)
  - Selected impact and destruction sounds.
- [Digital Audio](https://kenney.nl/assets/digital-audio)
  - Selected weapon and commander ability sounds.

The license files shipped with each Kenney pack are preserved under
`public/assets/licenses/`.
